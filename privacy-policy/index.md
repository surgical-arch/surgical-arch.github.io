# Privacy Policy for PRISM

**Effective Date: January 19, 2026**

PRISM ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in relation to your use of the PRISM Chrome extension.

### 1. Information Collection and Use
PRISM does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal information or browsing data to any external servers. All analysis is performed locally on your device/browser.

### 2. Data Security
Since no personal data is collected or stored, there is no risk of your personal information being leaked from our servers. We do not sell or share any user data with third parties.
